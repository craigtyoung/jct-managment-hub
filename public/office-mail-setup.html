Office inbox link

Shows front desk when new email lands in the shared office inbox. A small script inside that Gmail account tells the hub the sender, subject and time of unread mail. Email bodies never leave Gmail, and no Gmail password is used or stored here. Admins only.

Set it up (about ten minutes, once)
  1. Sign in to Gmail as the office account (jctennisoffice@gmail.com) in a browser.
  2. Open script.google.com → New project. Delete the sample code, click Copy script below, paste it in, and save (name it "JCT Hub inbox alert").
  3. Pick setup in the toolbar's function menu and click Run. Google asks for permission. Google will say "Google hasn't verified this app". That's normal for your own script: click Advanced → Go to JCT Hub inbox alert → Allow. Google's wording ("read, compose, send…") is its standard text for anything touching Gmail. This script only reads sender, subject and time, and you can read every line of it below.
  4. Come back here. The status above turns green within a minute or two.
  5. Send a test email to the office address. The dashboard card should show it within about a minute.
